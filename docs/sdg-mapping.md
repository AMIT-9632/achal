# SDG and VBYLD alignment

HSC-27 theme: **7. AI, GIS & Emerging Technologies** (primary), with **11. Disaster Mitigation &
Resilience** (GIS risk mapping, alerts, emergency response) and **5. Environment & Sustainability**
(environmental monitoring, coal-fire ground temperature) as secondary fits.

| SDG target | What it asks | How ACHAL contributes | KPI that shows it (`kpis.md`) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **11.5** | Reduce deaths, people affected and economic losses from disasters, with a focus on the poor and vulnerable | Warns households above fire/subsidence sites before collapse; most at-risk residents are non-title holders waiting for resettlement | Warning lead time, event recall, people covered per kit |
| **3.9** | Reduce deaths and illnesses from hazardous exposure and contamination | Ground-temperature and subsidence monitoring over coal fires flags spots where heat and gas come to the surface | Fire-scenario detection (W3), ground temperature per node |
| **8.8** | Safe and secure working environments for all workers | Same alarms protect surface mine workers, contract labour and drivers on haul roads | False alarms per node per month (trust), alert delivery |
| **9.1** | Quality, reliable, resilient infrastructure, with affordable and equitable access | Watches roads and rail over old workings; offline-first mesh keeps working with no internet; open hardware at ₹3,773 per node | Packet delivery with 30% nodes down, zero data loss during uplink cut, cost per node / per hectare |
| **13.1** | Strengthen resilience and adaptive capacity to climate-related hazards and natural disasters | Monsoon rain accelerates subsidence (largest InSAR swings May–September in Eastern Jharia); continuous monitoring through the monsoon when surveys stop | Uptime and battery autonomy through cloudy days |

Also relevant: **SDG 1.5** (build resilience of the poor to shocks) and **SDG 10.2** (inclusion):
alerts in Hindi, Bengali and English, with Santali under native-speaker review, and a siren that
needs no phone at all, reach Adivasi and migrant households that English-only, app-only systems miss.

## Viksit Bharat framing

- Coal still generates most of India's electricity and Jharia's coking coal feeds the steel sector;
  mining that does not cost lives or homes is part of a developed India's energy transition.
- Built by Jharkhand students for Jharkhand coalfields, open source (MIT), with Indian-sourced
  components, so CIL subsidiaries, DGMS and state agencies can deploy and extend it without
  licence fees.
